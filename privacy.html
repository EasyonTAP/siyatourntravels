At Siya World Holidays we're committed to protecting the privacy and security of our customers' personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website and travel services.

1. Introduction

We recognise the importance of protecting the privacy and security of your information and are committed to handling your data responsibly and transparently.

This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, and the measures we take to keep it secure during your travel booking and service experience with us.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number and billing details when you make a booking or interact with our website.

2.1 Personal Information

When you book travel services or create an account with us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, phone number and contact details
  • Billing information and payment details
  • Travel preferences and destination choices
  • Travel dates and accommodation preferences
  • Emergency contact information

2.2 Booking Information

We gather information related to your travel preferences to facilitate your booking experience:

  • Destination choices and travel dates
  • Accommodation and transportation preferences
  • Special requests or dietary requirements
  • Travel insurance preferences

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to process and manage your bookings, send confirmations and provide customer support throughout your journey.

  • Booking and reservation: Process and manage your bookings, send confirmations and provide customer support
  • Communication: Contact you via email or phone to update bookings and share relevant travel information
  • Service improvement: Analyse website performance, identify trends and enhance our services
  • Marketing: Inform you about promotions or special offers (only if you opt-in)
  • Legal compliance: Comply with legal obligations, enforce our terms of service and protect our rights

4. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We may share your information with trusted third-party partners who assist us in delivering travel services, but we do not sell or rent your personal information.

4.1 Third-party Service Providers

We share information with airlines, hotels, tour operators and payment processors. These partners have access only to the data required to fulfil your bookings and are not allowed to use it for other purposes.

4.2 Legal Requirements

We will share personal information when we have a good-faith belief that disclosure is reasonably necessary to:

  • Meet any applicable law, regulation or legal process
  • Enforce our terms of service
  • Detect, prevent or address fraud or security issues
  • Protect the rights, property or safety of Siya World Holidays, our customers or others

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction:

  • Secure data transmission using encryption protocols
  • Restricted access to personal information on a need-to-know basis
  • Regular security assessments and updates to our systems
  • Secure storage of payment and booking information

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information. You may:

  • Access and review the personal information we have collected about you
  • Request corrections to inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Restrict or object to certain processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices. We'll post the revised version with the effective date on our website. Your continued use of the website constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

For any questions, concerns or requests related to your privacy, please contact us using the details below.
