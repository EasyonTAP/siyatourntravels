1. Introduction

At Siya World Holidays we're committed to protecting the privacy and security of our customers' personal information. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we keep it secure.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Name, email, phone number and contact details

3. How We Use Your Information

4. Information Sharing

We share data with airlines, hotels, tour operators and payment processors to fulfil bookings. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We may disclose data when required by law.

5. Data Security

6. Your Rights

  • Restrict or object to certain processing

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the website constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
